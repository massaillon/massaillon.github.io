Major Achievement: CIE Partnership

Transforming ARM SYSTEMS' Business Through Energy Sector Diversification

Situation

ARM SYSTEMS had captured 100% of its existing target market in the telecom sector, with IHS Towers (managing all telecom towers in Ivory Coast) as its core client. This market saturation capped the company's growth potential, requiring a strategic pivot to new industries.

Task

As a newly promoted Business Developer, my primary objective was to hunt for and close a new, high-value partnership or deal to generate significant revenue outside of the saturated telecom sector. This required identifying a new industry with similar infrastructure management needs and developing a compelling value proposition for clients unfamiliar with our technology.

Action

I conducted extensive market research to identify companies with numerous, geographically dispersed sites, discovering that the National Electricity Company (CIE) managed over 10,000 electrical transformer sites across the country. After learning that CIE lost approximately 300 transformers annually to power overload with no real-time monitoring system, I developed a tailored telemetry solution proposal.

Before approaching CIE, I collaborated with our Technical Director to validate the technical feasibility, which led to a critical insight: upgrading the O-ring from 30A to 1250A to handle the electrical load. This technical validation ensured our proposal was sound and implementable.

I approached CIE with the tailored presentation, which immediately captured the attention of their director. To build trust and demonstrate value, we provided a Free Proof of Concept (POC) for several months. The successful POC convinced CIE to launch a formal RFP, inviting major industry giants like Schneider Electric, ABB, and Legrand. We submitted a highly targeted proposal based on the proven results of our POC.

Results

We successfully won the contract against established competition, securing a transformative 3-year deal with CIE in a completely new industry (energy), diversifying ARM SYSTEMS' revenue streams.
